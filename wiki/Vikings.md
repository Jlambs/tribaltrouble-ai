The **Vikings** are a crew of shipwrecked Norse raiders led by **Chief Fjorleif**. In the
story they wash up in the tropics after a storm and decide that the natives' golden statues
would make up for the loot they lost. They are loud, greedy and not very bright, and the
campaign dialogue plays this for laughs.

## In play

The Vikings' units and buildings have exactly the same stats as the [Natives'](Natives.md); see
[Tribes](Tribes.md). Their differences are:

- **A tougher Chieftain**: 60 hit points against the Natives' 40.
- **Lur magic.** Fjorleif's spells come from the *lur*, a long horn:
  - [Terrifying Toot](Magic.md#terrifying-toot) stuns every enemy within 36 for 10–30
    seconds and strips their defense, so your warriors hit them far more often.
  - [Ravaging Roar](Magic.md#ravaging-roar) blasts everything within 36 for at least 30
    damage, enough to kill any ordinary unit. It hits your own units and buildings too.
