# Strategy Guide

A starting point for new players, built on the game's actual rules (see
[Combat Mechanics](Combat-Mechanics.md) and [Economy](Economy.md)). Experienced players: please
add build orders, matchup notes and replays.

## The core ideas

1. **Peons are everything.** Every warrior is a peon who stopped working. Early on, more peons
   breeding and gathering beats a slightly bigger army.
2. **Breeding has diminishing returns, everything else doesn't.** A Quarters with 8 peons
   inside breeds twice as fast as one with 1, but one with 64 is only twice as fast as one with
   8. A second Quarters is usually a better use of peons than stuffing the first.
3. **Wood is the bottleneck.** Every building is wood, and every weapon costs 2 wood. Keep a
   steady stream of peons on trees all game.
4. **Weapon quality beats weapon count.** Units have 1 hit point, so fights come down to hit
   chance against defense. An iron warrior lands its throws on a rock warrior 37.5% of the time
   and gets hit back only 15% of the time.
5. **Fight from high ground.** Up to 25 extra percentage points of hit chance is huge. A rock
   warrior on a big hill trades evenly with an iron warrior below it.

## A simple opening

Starting with the default 20 peons:

1. Build a **Quarters** and an **Armory** at the same time, the Armory near trees and rock.
2. When the Quarters is done, put about a third of your peons inside to start breeding.
3. When the Armory is done, its builders go inside. Use *Gather Resources* to send most of them
   to wood and a few to rock.
4. Set the Quarters' **rally point** on the Armory, or leave it unset (new peons then walk into
   the nearest Armory by themselves), and keep moving newborn peons out to work.
5. Start rock weapon production with a few peons inside the Armory. Put the counter on infinity
   so it never stops.
6. Scout for **iron**, and build a second Armory next to it once your wood income is steady.

From there, the usual plan is a second Quarters, iron weapons, and a few towers on the paths
the enemy will use.

## Mid game

- **Switch to iron as soon as you can.** Iron weapons take twice the workshop time of rock
  weapons, but iron warriors survive far longer in a fight (70% defense against 50%).
- **Hunt chickens when you see them.** Only a few flocks are on the map at once. A handful of
  chicken warriors, with their bouncing throws, are very good against clumped enemies.
- **Train a Chieftain** once your Quarters has plenty of peons inside. Training costs no
  resources, only the Quarters' breeding time.
- **Towers** are cheap (20 wood) and hold one warrior who can't die until the tower falls. Put
  them over gathering spots and choke points, and man them with iron warriors.

## Attacking

- Use **Attack** on the ground (attack-move) rather than Move, so your army fights anything it
  meets on the way.
- Send **peons** at enemy towers. Their melee blows always deal 6 damage to a tower, so a group
  of peons wrecks a tower far faster than warriors throwing at it.
- Kill **peons** and the **Quarters** to stop the enemy's economy. In a standard game a tribe
  is defeated when it has no units, no Chieftain and no Quarters left.
- Use your Chieftain's spells at the moment of contact:
  - **Vikings:** Toot first (stunned units have no defense), then attack; or bring the
    Chieftain right into the enemy blob and Roar, with your own army held back.
  - **Natives:** drop the Stew where enemies will bunch up (a choke point, around their
    Armory); send the Cloud at a tower line or a camp.

## Defending

- Keep peons out of enemy throwing range; they have no defense at all.
- Put warriors on hills overlooking your base.
- Repair towers between waves (peons with wood, *Gather/Repair*).
- Watch the attack notifications. Press **Tab** to jump to the latest one.
