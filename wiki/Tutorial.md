# Tutorial

The tutorial is six short lessons that teach the controls and the economy. Start it from
**Main menu → Tutorial**. All six lessons are open from the start, in any order, and when one
ends you can go to the next lesson, restart, or return to the main menu.

In every lesson you play the [Natives](Natives.md) on a small island. Hints appear in a text
box; each step moves on as soon as you do what it asks. [Ships](Ship.md) are turned off.

| # | Lesson | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Getting around | Camera, map mode, selecting and moving units |
| 2 | The Quarters | Building, breeding peons, rally points |
| 3 | The Armory | Gathering, making weapons, deploying warriors |
| 4 | Constructing a tower | Towers, garrisons, repair |
| 5 | Training a chieftain | The Chieftain and magic |
| 6 | Defeating another tribe | A full small game against a passive opponent |

## 1. Getting around

You start with 20 peons and no enemy.

1. Pan by pushing the mouse against the screen edge, or with the arrow keys.
2. Zoom with the mouse wheel or Page Up / Page Down, or hold **Z** and move the mouse.
3. Rotate with numpad 4 / 6 or Insert / Delete.
4. Look up and down with numpad 2 / 8 or Home / End.
5. Hold **F** or the middle mouse button and move the mouse to turn the camera.
6. Press **Space** or numpad 5 to enter **Map Mode**, then click to jump there.
7. Select units by clicking, Shift-clicking, dragging a box, or double-clicking (selects all of
   that type on screen).
8. Move with the Move icon (**M**) or by right-clicking the ground.

## 2. The Quarters

1. Select peons, click **Quarters** (**Q**) and click a green square to place it.
2. Watch it go up. Numpad + / − change the game speed.
3. Move peons inside, and set a **Rally Point** (**R**).
4. Use the **+** icon to bring new peons out until you have 30 units.

## 3. The Armory

1. Build an **Armory** (**R**) and select it.
2. Open **Gather Resources** (**G**) and assign peons until you have 20 wood and 10 rock.
3. Go **Back** (**Esc**), open **Produce Weapons** (**W**) and make 10 rock weapons.
4. Go back, open **Deploy Army** (**A**) and train 10 rock warriors.

## 4. Constructing a tower

You start with 10 peons and one rock warrior.

1. Build a **Tower** (**T**) and right-click it with the warrior to put it inside.
2. A group of Viking rock warriors attacks. Watch the tower deal with them.
3. Repair the damage: select peons, use **Repair** (**G**) and click the tower.
4. Take the warrior out with **Exit** (**X**).

## 5. Training a chieftain

1. Build a Quarters, fill it with peons and click **Train Chieftain** (**C**). The Quarters
   makes no peons while training; pressing C again cancels.
2. When the Chieftain arrives, wait for each spell to charge and cast both.

## 6. Defeating another tribe

Everything together: build a Quarters and an Armory, gather, make weapons, deploy an army,
then select it, use **Attack** (**A**) and click on the enemy across the island. The lesson
ends when the Viking tribe has no units or buildings left.

## After the tutorial

Try a skirmish against the Easy AI (see [Game Setup](Game-Setup.md)), then the
[Viking Campaign](Viking-Campaign.md). The [Strategy Guide](Strategy-Guide.md) covers what the
tutorial doesn't.
