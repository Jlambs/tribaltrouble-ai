# Tribal Trouble Wiki

**Tribal Trouble** is a real-time strategy game set on a chain of tropical islands, where a tribe
of **[Vikings](Vikings.md)** washes ashore and runs into the local **[Natives](Natives.md)**. It
was released by the Danish studio Oddlabs in 2004, open-sourced under the GPL v2 in 2014, and
is kept alive today by a community fork released on Steam as **Tribal Trouble: Resurrected**.

The game is small and fast to learn. There are two tribes, four buildings, five unit types,
four resources and four spells, and most of the depth comes from how you run your economy and
where you fight. This wiki collects the rules, the numbers behind them, and the community's
know-how.

## Ways to play

- **[Campaigns](Campaigns.md)**: the [Viking Campaign](Viking-Campaign.md) and the
  [Native Campaign](Native-Campaign.md), played island by island on Easy, Normal or Hard.
- **Skirmish against the AI**: any map, any tribe, see [Game Setup](Game-Setup.md).
- **[Multiplayer](Multiplayer.md)**: online matchmaking with chat rooms, rated games and a
  leaderboard. You can also [spectate](Spectating.md) games in progress.
